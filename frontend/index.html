Autonomous AI Privacy & SQL Firewall Gateway

AdminZero (engineered by GlitchGo) is a next-generation security perimeter built for modern AI agents, LLM applications, and enterprise databases. While legacy firewalls rely on easily bypassed regex string filters, AdminZero mathematically decomposes incoming queries into strict Abstract Syntax Trees (AST), validates semantic intent, scrubs sensitive PII, and quenches prompt injection or database exfiltration attacks before they touch your database.

Why this challenge exists: We believe mathematical AST defense is unbreakable. We placed a secret flag behind this live 12-layer perimeter and opened it to the global hacker community to prove our architecture. Extract the flag to earn lifetime enterprise access and public recognition.
